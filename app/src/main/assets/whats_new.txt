# Shown once, the first time a new version opens (not on a fresh install). Keep it to the few things worth knowing.
# Lines starting with "- " are the points; the first line without "#" or "-" is the heading.
Quicker source switching, and sound that comes back
- Live TV: switching source keeps the channel playing, then swaps in the new stream in a second or two; stalled streams reconnect by themselves
- Films and shows: Match frame rate switches the TV as they open (frame rate from Plex / Jellyfin / Emby), so the sound works from the start
- Auto-play match: pick which addon stream plays (4K, Dolby Vision, Atmos…), sorted by importance (Settings → Playback)
- Transparency that does what it says (0–100%), a clock on the info bar, and your provider shown on it
