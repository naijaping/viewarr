# Shown once, the first time a new version opens (not on a fresh install). Keep it to the few things worth knowing.
# Lines starting with "- " are the points; the first line without "#" or "-" is the heading.
A new button layout, and a choice of play order
- Live TV: Up / Down now change channel; the TV guide's Left carries on into catch-up (Settings → Remote buttons → presets)
- Button settings can't break the remote any more: OK, Back and Search always keep working
- Play order: your servers, IPTV, then addons (Settings → Playback → Streams); hold OK on Play lists every source
- Three more info bar styles and a longer TV guide timeline (Settings → Appearance)
