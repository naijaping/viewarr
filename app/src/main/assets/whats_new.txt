A new look, My Library, and Live now in the guide
- A new logo, and the menu takes the page's own background
- My Library shows your own servers' films and shows (choose its rows in Settings)
- Live now is a group in the TV guide, with real live sport only
- EPG offset is a slider per channel, and the guide's Timeline is in its hold-OK menu
